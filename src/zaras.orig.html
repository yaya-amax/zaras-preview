Who We Are

A Family Table,
Open to All

Zara's Mediterranean Kitchen brings the traditional taste of authentic Mediterranean cuisine to the Woodlands area. Our philosophy is simple: healthy, homemade, nutritious, mouthwatering family recipes built on love, health, family and community.

Everything is prepared daily — from garden-fresh vegetables and herbs to local organic meats, authentic Eastern spices, healthy fats and olive oil. These are grandmother's recipes, passed down and served the way they were meant to be.
